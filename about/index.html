About us

Your local dental team

Family-run care in Currambine, for patients of every age.

Sunlander Dental, Currambine Perth

As one of Perth's general dental clinics, Sunlander Dental Centre provides an extensive range of dental services, led by our dedicated team of dental surgeons and assistants. Coupled with quality equipment, resources, and training, we pride ourselves as a quality, affordable clinic. As a family-run practice, we aim to provide all our patients with the level of care we would offer to our own family and friends.

Here at Sunlander Dental Centre, we are not happy until you are.

Whether it be cracked teeth, missing teeth, discoloured teeth, or wisdom teeth, we offer a full range of dental treatments to cover general oral health needs. We love children, so please feel free to bring your little ones along. For your convenience, we offer instant health insurance claim rebates using HICAPS. We also offer Saturday appointment times and obligation-free quotes during your initial appointment. We are also part of a busy medical centre, and take a holistic approach to your oral health.

Dentists

Dr Hetal Hirpara

Dentist, Female, BDS, ADC Certificate

Dr Hetal Hirpara graduated from dental school in India in 2009. After completing her degree, she worked for a few years in India before moving to Australia in 2017 when she got married to Dr Ravi Haria. In 2020 after successfully completing the ADC exam she started her dental career at Sunlander Dental Centre. Dr Hetal is known for her gentle and perfectionist approach for all aspects of dentistry with all age groups and treating them with genuine care and reassurance. Dr Hetal performs general dentistry with a holistic approach to treatment, she enjoys doing cosmetic dentistry and believes in a preventative approach to dentistry. She also believes in educating and motivating the patient to make the best decision for their dental and general health care for which she keeps herself updated through continuing professional education (CPD). Dr Hetal is a member of the Australian Dental Association (ADA) and she is a certified Invisalign Go provider. In her free time Dr Hetal loves gardening, cooking, painting and reading.

Languages: English, Gujarati, Hindi, and Marathi

Dr Ravi Haria

Dentist, Male, BDS, ADC Certificate, MPH

Dr Ravi Haria has been a dentist at Sunlander Dental Centre since 2014. He has always believed in treating people, not just teeth. This reflects in his passion towards comprehensive and preventative care for his patients and their families. He is approachable and available to answer any queries or concerns about oral health. He strives to enhance his professional skills through continuing professional education to provide the highest standard of care. His time outside the dental office is spent in the kitchen trying new curries, catching up with cricket, and gardening.
