Sunlander Dental, Currambine Perth

As one of Perth's general dental clinics, Sunlander Dental Centre provides an extensive range of dental services, led by our dedicated team of dental surgeons and assistants. Coupled with quality equipment, resources, and training, we pride ourselves as a quality, affordable clinic. As a family-run practice, we aim to provide all our patients with the level of care we would offer to our own family and friends.

Here at Sunlander Dental Centre, we are not happy until you are.

Whether it be cracked teeth, missing teeth, discoloured teeth, or wisdom teeth, we offer a full range of dental treatments to cover general oral health needs. We love children, so please feel free to bring your little ones along. For your convenience, we offer instant health insurance claim rebates using HICAPS. We also offer Saturday appointment times and obligation-free quotes during your initial appointment. We take a holistic approach to your oral health.

Your local team

A practice you will want to come back to.

Located in Currambine, Sunlander Dental Centre is a family friendly practice where new patients are always welcome.

Many people don't see a dentist on a regular basis. They go only when they feel they have a problem. While that may feel like saving money, it usually costs more in both dollars and time, because most dental problems don't have symptoms until they reach the advanced stages.

We want to help you maintain your oral health, and we strive to provide a comfortable and reassuring atmosphere so that you will want to return on a regular basis. If you are new to the area, or haven't been to a dentist for a while, contact the team and book your appointment.
